## Intent

A launch-reveal promo for the just-redesigned calebpierre.com — Caleb Pierre,
a Business Automation Engineer in Los Angeles. The site relaunched in a bold
white/black/red palette (pulled straight from his own business card) with
7 services: Web Design, AI Programming, Business Automation, Content
Marketing, Remote Tech Support, Experience/Event Design, and Cybersecurity.
This is a sell/market video (not a neutral site tour) — it should feel like
a startup product-launch drop: confident, punchy, a little bit of "this just
went live" energy.

Copy must be plain English at an 8th-grade reading level — dead simple,
direct-response VSL style. Short sentences. No jargon. Every line should
land on first watch with zero re-reading. Think "read it out loud to a
12-year-old and they get it," not corporate-speak.

Real proof points to draw on (from the site's actual case studies):
Eden's Candy (brand + store redesign), Hummingbyrd Apparel & Print
(end-to-end Shopify build), OutsideTonight (live AI/automation product).

## Customizations

- Reveal structure: cold open on the "CP" mark → bold headline reveal
  ("I'm your Business Automation Engineer") → one fast flash-montage beat
  covering all 7 services (not 7 separate beats — confirmed fix for fitting
  a square 45s cut) → linger on 2-3 real project proof shots → the offer →
  CTA.
- **Offer, must appear on-screen AND in the voiceover, clearly and
  confidently: a $250 website special, new clients only, tied to the
  relaunch as a limited/can't-miss deal.** This is a required beat, not
  optional decoration — the video does not work without it landing clearly.
- Design spec: use the site's own captured brand (white/black/red, Inter +
  JetBrains Mono) rather than a shipped preset — this is a real business's
  real brand, not a generic look.
- Audio: AI voiceover reads the VSL-style script, music bed underneath.

## Notes

- Site to capture: https://calebpierre.com (also runnable locally via
  `npm run dev` in the parent calebpierre.com repo if live capture is
  unavailable).
- Avoid: corporate jargon, long sentences, anything that requires a second
  read. Keep the $250 offer easy to spot and impossible to miss.
