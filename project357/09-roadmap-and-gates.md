# CPT 90-Day Activation Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Legibility and control, Days 1–14

- Confirm CPT as a CPV brand and document any DBA requirement.
- Open and configure the CPV business account.
- Begin back-tax and bookkeeping remediation.
- Verify lease, use, insurance, permits, utilities, and facility constraints.
- Secure ACE support letter, conflict disclosure, and draft MOU.
- Prepare capability statement, service menu, pilot brief, and evidence portfolio.
- Meet SBWIB Business Services/Career Pathways 180 and JCOD contacts.

**Gate:** Legal entity, money flow, location hypothesis, partner roles, and owner are legible.

## Phase 1 — Revenue before buildout, Days 15–30

- Pre-sell three deployment or media engagements.
- Collect deposits into the company account.
- Submit responsible CDFI applications with use-of-funds plan.
- Confirm first cohort referral source and administrator.
- Price minimum viable furniture, network, media, and computing equipment.

**Gate:** At least three paid engagements or equivalent contracted revenue; no speculative equipment spree.

## Phase 2 — Minimum viable hub, Days 31–60

- Activate secure network, modular lab, product station, and basic media capability.
- Test booking, payment, access, inventory, incident, and reset procedures.
- Deliver commercial work from the site.
- Finalize pilot curriculum, wage/stipend mechanism, participant consent, and outcome plan.

**Gate:** Hub passes operational, safety, security, revenue, and program-readiness checks.

## Phase 3 — First cohort and public proof, Days 61–90

- Enroll 6–10 adults through approved partners.
- Run baseline, attendance, supervised project, and weekly risk reviews.
- Hold employer demonstration day.
- Produce aggregate outcome report and financial reconciliation.
- Decide: repeat, revise, expand, pause, or archive.

**Gate:** Evidence supports a second cohort and continued facility investment.

## Next horizon

- I-TRAIN/ETPL readiness.
- OJT and registered apprenticeship exploration.
- Recurring small-business clinic and deployment retainer base.
- Direct public contracting only after compliance and delivery evidence mature.
