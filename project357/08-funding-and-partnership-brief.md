# CPT Funding and Partnership Brief

## Public narrative

Caleb Pierre Technologies is a community technology incubator, workforce training space, and digital media hub in Compton. CPT helps residents and neighborhood businesses gain practical access to software, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, information science, digital production, and modern business infrastructure.

The hub converts training into tangible outcomes: deployed products, paid experience, portfolios, references, stronger businesses, jobs, contracts, and participant-owned ventures.

## Partnership architecture

| Partner | Proposed responsibility |
|---|---|
| CPT | Facility, technical curriculum, instructors, supervised projects, commercial demand |
| ACE | Nonprofit prime, grant administration, community outreach, compliance, case coordination |
| SBWIB/AJCC | Eligibility, referrals, OJT/work experience, employer services, CalJOBS reporting |
| JCOD/SECTOR | Reentry-specific referrals, paid pathways, supportive-service alignment |
| Halfway houses | Trusted recruitment, schedule coordination, participant support |
| Employers | Job requirements, demonstrations, interviews, placements, sponsorship |
| City/community partners | Visibility, referrals, facility support, public alignment |

## Capital stack

1. Customer deposits and retainers.
2. CDFI working-capital/equipment financing.
3. Workforce contracts and paid work-experience reimbursements.
4. Foundation and community grants administered by eligible partners.
5. Equipment sponsorship and in-kind support.
6. Earned studio, training, deployment, and membership revenue.

No single source should control hub survival.

## Initial funding request

Fund facility activation, safe equipment, connectivity, instruction, participant wages/stipends, case coordination, insurance, software, and outcome reporting for a bounded pilot.

## Funder outcomes

- Residents enrolled and completing.
- Paid work hours and measurable skill gains.
- Deployed projects and verified portfolios.
- Businesses receiving usable digital assets.
- Employment, earnings, retention, contracts, and ventures after exit.
- Cost per participant and cost per successful outcome.

## Partner pitch

“CPT provides the place, technical system, instructors, and production environment. The workforce partner provides eligibility, supportive services, and public-program compliance. Employers provide demand and opportunity. Together, the partnership turns access into evidence and evidence into income.”
