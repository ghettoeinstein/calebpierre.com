# CPT Hub Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product definition

The CPT Hub is a modular physical facility where a visitor can learn a skill, build a product, deploy it, document it, produce its media, and connect it to income.

## 2. Primary users

- Local small-business owner needing a digital presence or workflow.
- Justice-impacted adult seeking a credible technology pathway.
- Founder or creator producing a prototype and launch package.
- Employer evaluating trained talent.
- Workforce or public partner purchasing measurable outcomes.
- CPT operator managing programs, access, equipment, evidence, and revenue.

## 3. Jobs to be done

- “Help me turn an idea or business need into a working digital product.”
- “Help me prove I can perform professional technical work.”
- “Help me create professional product and media assets locally.”
- “Help me fund, refer, supervise, and verify workforce outcomes.”
- “Help me see what CPT is producing for Hub City.”

## 4. Physical zones

### A. Technology and workflow lab

Modular desks, secure network, presentation display, demo island, device-testing bench, lockable equipment storage, and flexible instruction layout.

### B. Media and podcast studio

Two-to-four-person recording layout, controllable acoustic treatment, prewired camera positions, safe lighting, and rapid reset between sessions.

### C. Product and e-commerce station

Neutral backdrops, tabletop sweep, product lighting, overhead capture, turntable, and staging storage.

### D. Prototype station

Enclosed, supervised 3D printing using lower-risk materials during v1. Any expansion into lasers, resin, fumes, or industrial fabrication requires a separate safety RFC, landlord approval, ventilation design, and insurance review.

### E. Quiet operations zone

Private partner meetings, case-management conversations, secure records, and focused engineering work.

## 5. Functional requirements

- Book and authorize rooms, equipment, instructors, and programs.
- Distinguish public, participant, staff, partner, and production access.
- Track equipment custody, condition, maintenance, and funding source.
- Capture attendance, milestones, deliverables, consent, and outcomes.
- Provide segmented wired and wireless networks.
- Protect client, participant, and credential data.
- Support hybrid instruction and demonstrations.
- Reset each zone rapidly for its next revenue or program use.
- Produce funder-ready and customer-ready evidence without exposing private data.

## 6. Safety and accessibility

- ADA-accessible circulation and workstations.
- Posted occupancy, emergency, incident, and evacuation procedures.
- No minors in adult reentry cohorts; youth programming requires a separate safeguarding policy.
- Supervision for prototyping equipment.
- Cable, electrical-load, fire, ventilation, and insurance review before opening.
- Quiet private intake; conviction history is not displayed or used as branding material.

## 7. Launch acceptance gates

The hub does not open publicly until:

- Lease and permitted use are verified.
- General and professional liability coverage is active.
- Network segmentation and credential handling are tested.
- Emergency and incident procedures are posted.
- Equipment inventory is complete.
- Booking, payment, refund, consent, and privacy terms are published.
- At least three paid commercial engagements or equivalent contracted revenue are scheduled.
- The first community program has an accountable administrator and funding source.
