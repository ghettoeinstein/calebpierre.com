# Caleb Pierre Technologies Charter

## 1. Identity

Caleb Pierre Technologies is the technology operating brand of Caleb Pierre Ventures LLC. CPT is headquartered conceptually and physically in Compton—Hub City—and exists to make advanced technical capability locally accessible, economically productive, and institutionally durable.

**Positioning:** A physical operating system for technology in Hub City.

## 2. Mission

CPT equips residents, justice-impacted adults, creators, founders, and neighborhood businesses to learn, build, deploy, secure, document, and earn through technology and information science.

## 3. Public promise

CPT converts technology access into:

- Deployed digital products.
- Paid and portfolio-quality experience.
- Stronger local businesses.
- Defensible technical skills.
- Employment, contracting, and entrepreneurship pathways.
- Locally owned intellectual and productive assets.

## 4. Scope

CPT may operate:

- Software, AI, cybersecurity, cloud, and automation services.
- A supervised open technology lab.
- Workforce training and work-based learning.
- Product, e-commerce, podcast, and digital-media production.
- Small-product prototyping and demonstrations.
- Research, documentation, information science, and public-interest technology programs.
- Venture experiments governed by written manifests and budgets.

## 5. Beneficiaries and customers

CPT serves two complementary markets:

1. Paying customers: small businesses, institutions, creators, government partners, nonprofits, and venture clients.
2. Community participants: residents and job seekers who need access, instruction, evidence of skill, and pathways to income.

Commercial revenue sustains the hub. Public and philanthropic funding expands access. Neither side may obscure the other.

## 6. Participant rights

Every participant has the right to:

- Clear program terms, costs, expectations, and outcomes.
- Safe, respectful, accessible instruction.
- Privacy and control over public use of their identity or story.
- Compensation when performing productive client work.
- Accurate attribution for their contributions.
- A process to report concerns without retaliation.
- Exportable evidence of completed work and acquired skills.

## 7. Non-delegable founder powers

The owner retains authority over:

- Mission, brand, intellectual-property strategy, and venture admission.
- Treasury ceilings and material financial commitments.
- Opening or closing programs and locations.
- Production access to sensitive systems.
- Final approval of partnerships that change CPT’s obligations or control.

These powers may be supported by agents and staff but cannot be silently transferred.

## 8. Invariants

1. Entity funds, records, contracts, and accounts remain separated.
2. Grant money is restricted to its approved purpose.
3. No person approves a transaction from which they improperly benefit.
4. No participant is used as unpaid production labor.
5. Sensitive credentials use least privilege and are never shared casually.
6. Models may propose; accountable humans approve consequential actions.
7. Claims of impact require evidence.
8. Every venture has an owner, budget, purpose, review date, and stop condition.
9. The hub prioritizes useful production over performative activity.
10. Safety, consent, privacy, and legal requirements outrank speed.

## 9. Economic doctrine

CPT ventures should progressively pay for themselves, operate themselves, and finance additional productive assets. Experiments begin bounded, earn expanded authority through evidence, and are archived when they fail to justify continued attention or capital.

## 10. Amendment

Material amendments require a written version, rationale, impact assessment, effective date, and founder approval. Grant- or partner-specific requirements may add constraints but cannot silently weaken this Charter.
